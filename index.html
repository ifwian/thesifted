Our story

We sift out the rush and keep the good moments.

A cozy neighborhood cafe made for slow mornings, good conversations, and cups worth coming back for. Whether you are here to relax, meet friends, get some work done, or enjoy a good meal, there is a seat with your name on it.

  • Thoughtfully roasted

    Beans sourced and roasted with care, brewed the way they deserve to be.

  • Made fresh daily

    Pastries and food prepared fresh each morning.

  • A place to stay awhile

    Comfortable seating and a calm atmosphere, whether you are working or catching up.

  • Warm, familiar service

    A team that genuinely enjoys having you here.
